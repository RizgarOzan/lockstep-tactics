lockstep-tactics — a match you can verify

source & notes
  • Play Blue. Press play Blue, click one of your units (or its card), then a tile to move or an orange unit to attack. Orange is the built-in AI. Pause with space to think; orders land on the next tick.
  • Soldier beats Archer, Archer beats Knight, Knight beats Soldier. Archers shoot four tiles and are weak up close; Knights are slow and hard to kill.
  • The hash is the proof. The number in the corner is the state hash after this tick. MSVC on Windows, clang on an ARM Mac and this WebAssembly build all print the same one for the same seed and orders.
  • Save the replay. save replay downloads the seed, every order and every tick's hash; lockstep verify checks it on any machine and names the first tick that differs.

Fixed-point C++20 compiled to WebAssembly with raylib. Nothing here uses float except the drawing. Why that matters.
